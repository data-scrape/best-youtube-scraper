# YouTube Business Email Scraper

A YouTube business email scraper helps identify publicly displayed creator contact information for legitimate business outreach.

[CoreClaw](https://www.coreclaw.com/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=L7&utm_term=&utm_id=L7) supports creator research and the collection of publicly available business information from relevant public sources.

## Possible Data Fields

Depending on public availability, results may include:

- Channel name
- Channel URL
- Business email
- External website
- Social links
- Channel description
- Country
- Subscriber count
- Video count

## Common Use Cases

- Influencer sponsorship outreach
- Brand partnerships
- Affiliate recruitment
- Creator collaboration
- Agency prospecting
- Public relations outreach

## Responsible Outreach

Users should:

- Contact creators only for relevant business purposes
- Respect opt-out requests
- Avoid unsolicited bulk messaging
- Follow applicable email and privacy laws
- Use only publicly available business contact information
